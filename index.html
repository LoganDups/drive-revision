What you can say

Any timeYou don't have to wait. Talk over the question or the explanation and it stops and acts straight away.
“A” … “E”Answer. If a letter is misheard, use “Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo”, or “option B”.
“Explain”Full step-by-step reasoning. Works before or after you answer. “I don't know” does the same.
“Repeat”Read the question and options again. “Options” reads just the choices.
“Scenario”Re-read the shared table or case information.
“Next”Move to the next question (or skip this one).
“Pause”Go quiet. Say “Resume” to carry on.
“Score”Hear how you're doing.
“Slower” / “Faster”Change reading speed.
“End session”Stop and hear a summary.

Set everything up and press Start before you pull away. The screen stays on so the phone keeps listening. Mount it and don't look at it while driving.
